1. The incident in my own words

The Solarwinds attack was a cyberattack where hackers got into SolarWinds software. They secretly added harmful code to a software update. Customers downloaded the update normally and did not know that it also gave the hackers access to their systems.

2. Who was affected, and how?

Many companies and government organisations were affected by the attack. The hackers were able to enter systems and access information without being noticed. The attack also caused problems for the US government and became a serious international security issue.

3. The CIA principle

I think Confidentiality was affected the most because the hackers got access to private information and systems. Integrity was also affected because the hackers changed trusted software by adding their own code. Availability was not the main problem because the hackers were mainly trying to stay hidden and collect information.

4. The attack technique

A supply chain attack is when hackers attack a company that other organisations trust and use. In this case, SolarWinds software was used by many organisations, so the hackers could reach many targets through one company. This made the attack much bigger than attacking each organisation separately.

5. How was it discovered?

The attack was discovered when the security company FireEye found something suspicious in its own systems. They realised that someone had gained access using a fake authentication method. This shows that cyberattacks can stay hidden for a long time before someone notices them.

6. What could have helped?

Better security monitoring could have helped find the attack earlier. Strong passwords, MFA and limiting access could also have made it harder for the hackers to move around systems. Regular checks of software updates could have helped as well.

7. The broader lesson

The main lesson for me is that even software from a trusted company can become a security problem. Organisations also need to think about the security of the companies and software they depend on. Security is not only about protecting your own systems.

8. My personal takeaway

I will think more carefully about the software and updates I use. Before this, I mostly thought of updates as something that automatically made software safer. The SolarWinds attack showed me that a trusted update can still contain something harmful if the company providing it has been attacked.
